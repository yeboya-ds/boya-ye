About关于

Trained as a landscape architect (MLA, University of Pennsylvania; LEED AP). Later brought site narrative into product and data interfaces—websites, tools, and dashboards for emergency management. The through-line: who a place or product is for, how the story is seen, and how data helps people decide.景观设计出身，宾夕法尼亚大学景观建筑硕士，LEED AP。后来把场地叙事带到产品与数据界面：网站、工具，以及服务应急管理的仪表盘。贯穿始终的是：这块地或这个产品为谁而做，故事如何被看见，数据如何帮人做决定。

Currently crafting tools and dashboards that blend AI and data for emergency management.目前在做融合 AI 与数据的应急管理工具与仪表盘。
